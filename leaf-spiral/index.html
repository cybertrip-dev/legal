Leaf Spiral — Support

Thanks for playing Leaf Spiral, a spiral climb that paints the trunk as it goes. Drag to spin the trunk while the sprout climbs on its own — spinning faster climbs faster and winds the spiral tighter, so the only tactical resource is spinning slowly. A column only paints rows it has not visited, and the instant the trunk completes a full turn in one direction the whole open band confirms at once and buys back wilt time.
