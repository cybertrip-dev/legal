Ramen Drop — Support

Thanks for playing Ramen Drop, a physics game about assembling a bowl of ramen to order. A noodle machine tracks back and forth overhead. Flick fast to cut the dough ribbon, cut the strings holding the toppings, and tap either side of the screen to tilt the bowl and catch what falls. Tilting past a threshold spills broth, and the broth you spill is exactly what a three-star bowl needs, so widening the catch is a bill you choose to pay — the alternative is timing your taps to the wave already sloshing in the bowl so the noodles fold themselves in. The ticket at the top says what the bowl must contain; SERVE is always available, and scores what you have.
