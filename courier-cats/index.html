Courier Cats — Support

Thanks for playing Courier Cats, a three-lane delivery runner. A cat courier runs a three-lane street and stacks parcels on its back. You choose where a parcel goes on, but only the top ever comes off. Awnings shear away everything above their clearance line, and post boxes ask for a count and a type, so a jam is never about being too tall — it is about the order you stacked in.
