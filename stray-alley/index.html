STRAY ALLEY — Support

Thanks for playing STRAY ALLEY, a one-tap idle cat-photography collection game. Tap the food dish to draw stray cats into the alley, then tap a visiting cat the moment it yawns or stretches to snap a photo. Better timing earns a better shot, and every cat you photograph is added to your album.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email us at info@cybertrip.jp. We usually reply within a few business days.
