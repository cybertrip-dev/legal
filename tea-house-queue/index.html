Tea House Queue — Support

Thanks for playing Tea House Queue, a two-minute shop-routing puzzle. Place stepping stones one at a time to lay out both the path guests walk in on and the path the hostess serves along. Guests and hostess can only walk on stones and only one person fits per stone, so the elegant single-file path you just drew is the same jam you will be standing in.
