Stair Spin — Support

Thanks for playing Stair Spin, a one-tap climb up a tower of turning gear floors. Every floor is one read: a marker shows exactly which slot a tap would drop you into, so you wait for it to line up, swipe a slot around the ring to make the angle yourself, or hold the core to freeze your own rotation and re-phase — all under a gear-shift deadline that starts the instant you land.
