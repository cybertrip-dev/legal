Moonlight Laundry — Support

Thanks for playing Moonlight Laundry, a one-finger sorting and timing game. A coin laundry lit only by moonlight. You decide when to start each of the three washers, so the rate work arrives at is in your hands rather than the game's. Laundry that overflows onto the floor is not a loss — a downward swipe puts it back on the pile — so a hundred-second shift is about when to borrow time and when to pay it back rather than about reflexes.
