Pocket Orchestra — Support

Thanks for playing Pocket Orchestra, a composing puzzle about arranging songbirds along a branch. Each bird sings whatever perch it is sitting on, so rearranging the birds rewrites an eight-step loop. Scoring is not a snapshot of one pass but the average of four, because the migratory birds carry an arrow and shift one perch to the right on every repeat — there is no such thing as a good single bar, only a good four bars. A running four-pass line sits under the board so the consequence of a move is visible immediately. Nothing asks you to read music or to play in time; the only inputs are dragging and tapping.
