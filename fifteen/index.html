FIFTEEN — Support

Thanks for playing FIFTEEN, a one-tap neon sliding-puzzle game. Tap a tile in the same row or column as the empty square and the tiles between them slide over, snapping the fragments of a shattered photo back into place. Solve each board in fewer taps than par to earn a sub-par bonus.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email us at info@cybertrip.jp. We usually reply within a few business days.
