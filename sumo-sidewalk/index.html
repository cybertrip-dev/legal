Sumo Sidewalk — Support

Thanks for playing Sumo Sidewalk, a swipe-and-tap sidewalk runner. Run three lanes of pavement and decide which oncoming wrestler to meet head-on. There is exactly one way to gain weight — win a clash and take a third of the loser's mass — and exactly one way to shed it, a charge that did not win. Both come out of the same tap, so every tap is a bet on whether you want to be heavier or narrower. Weight raises your score multiplier and widens your body until the gaps stop fitting.
