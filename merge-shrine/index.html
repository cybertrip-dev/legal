Merge Shrine — Support

Thanks for playing Merge Shrine, a merge puzzle about repairing a small shrine from the offering backwards. Fifteen materials across three families and five levels. Tapping the source spends one spirit and drops a level-one piece; dragging a piece onto its twin merges it and pays spirit back, and the higher the merge the more it pays. Because a wand can split a piece back into two of the level below, the cheap way to reach what a votive tablet is asking for is to build one level too high and then split down — every other rule in the game follows from that one. Nothing is ever destroyed by a mis-drag: a mismatched drop simply swaps the two pieces.
