EMERGE — Support

Thanks for playing EMERGE, a one-tap pattern-recognition arcade game. Watch 280 scattered dots drift together into the silhouette of a hidden beast, tap the instant you recognize it, then pick that beast from four choices. The earlier you commit the more you score, and three wrong guesses end the run.
