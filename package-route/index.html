Package Route — Support

Thanks for playing Package Route, a route-planning delivery game with no steering. You draw one day's delivery route with a finger and press GO; once the van is moving there is no steering at all. The whole turn is deciding how to spend the 480 minutes between 08:00 and 16:00. A block becomes yours only when two things hold at once — your route encloses it on all four sides, and every house facing it has been delivered — so a there-and-back branch never claims territory and a loop does. While you plan, the simulation re-runs on every movement of your finger and the display shows what would happen if you drove it exactly as drawn.
