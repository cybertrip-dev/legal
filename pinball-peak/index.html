PINBALL PEAK — Support

Thanks for playing PINBALL PEAK, a one-flick slingshot climbing game. Pull back anywhere on the screen and release to slingshot your pinball up a neon mountain of glowing ledges. Land cleanly to build your combo, avoid the bumpers that knock you back down, and climb as high as you can before the 45-second timer runs out.
