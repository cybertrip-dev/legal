Pocket Planet Post — Support

Thanks for playing Pocket Planet Post, a one-stroke mail route puzzle with a last-in-first-out hold. You draw one unbroken line without lifting your finger and call at every world in the system exactly once. The ship's hold is a stack: parcels pile on top of each other and the only one you can ever unload is the one on top. So what you are really designing is not the shape of the line but the nesting order of pickups and deliveries — the smallest correct form is collect A, collect B, deliver B, deliver A. Judgement lands the instant you lift your finger, and a solved system is discarded for a freshly generated one.
