Garden Squares — Support

Thanks for playing Garden Squares, a dots-and-boxes garden puzzle. Join dots of the same colour and close a loop into a box, and that entire colour leaves the board. Only the soil the box covered gets watered, and soil watered three times blooms. Meet the quota of flowers inside the move limit and the plot clears; fall short and the run ends there.
