WATERWAY — Support

Thanks for playing WATERWAY, a one-tap neon pipe-routing puzzle game. Tap a pipe segment to rotate it 90 degrees and link an unbroken line from the source at the top of the board to the drain at the bottom. Clear each board before the tap gauge runs out, and waste no taps to keep your combo multiplier climbing.
