Pulse Rails — Support

Thanks for playing Pulse Rails, a five-rail current-grazing arcade run. The thing that threatens you and the thing that pays you are the same shape: a white core that kills on contact wrapped in a halo that banks charge. Graze the front of a pulse, cross to the next rail before the core arrives, then slip into the rear halo the instant the white has passed — land both halves and the pulse turns around and comes back worth more.
