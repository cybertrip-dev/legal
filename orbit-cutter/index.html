Orbit Cutter — Support

Thanks for playing Orbit Cutter, a vertical climb where cutting is the only way through. Your craft is fixed to the centre of the lane and rises without stopping, and there is no dodge input at all — the rotating arms blocking the way have to be cut with a fast swipe. The pieces you cut off do not vanish: each half leaves along the tangent it was travelling, so the phase you cut at decides where the debris goes, and cutting at the wrong moment throws your own wreckage straight back into the corridor you are about to climb into. Reading the rotation and waiting for the phase that flings the halves clear is the whole skill.
