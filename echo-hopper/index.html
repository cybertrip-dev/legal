Echo Hopper — Support

Thanks for playing Echo Hopper, a one-thumb grid-hopping arcade runner. Hop north across a seven-column grid with a virtual stick while every move you make is recorded. At each zone boundary you step on one of three plates to choose how that recording comes back — mirrored, shifted or delayed — and it returns as an echo running the other way. The only hazards are copies of yourself, none of them can be erased, and you score by swallowing an echo inside its brief absorbing window and welding it onto the next one.
