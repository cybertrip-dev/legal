CASH OUT — Support

Thanks for playing CASH OUT, a one-tap press-your-luck arcade game. Your rocket climbs on its own and the multiplier accelerates the longer you ride, but every round hides a crash point you cannot see. Tap to cash out before it blows, bank the multiplier, and keep compounding round after round.
