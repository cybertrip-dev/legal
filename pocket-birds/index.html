Pocket Birds — Support

Thanks for playing Pocket Birds, a placement puzzle about attracting birds. Place feeders, perches, water and cover on a garden grid to satisfy several bird species at once while the daylight runs down. The species want incompatible things, so a single layout has to answer all of them rather than simply score high.
