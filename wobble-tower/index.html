WOBBLE TOWER — Support

Thanks for playing WOBBLE TOWER, a one-tap neon block-stacking game. Tap to drop the sweeping neon block straight down and stack your tower toward the moon. Every off-center block makes the tower lean, so stack matching colors to merge them into a heavy counterweight and pull the tower back upright.
