MINEFIELD — Support

Thanks for playing MINEFIELD, a one-tap retro-CRT minesweeper game. Tap a cell to open it, read the numbers to work out which neighboring cells hide mines, and clear every safe cell without stepping on one. Every board is generated so it can be solved by logic alone, so there is never a guess.

Contact
