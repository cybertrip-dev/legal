Ice Carve — Support

Thanks for playing Ice Carve, a one-stroke carving puzzle. Carve a channel through a floating ice block so the fish trapped inside slides out to sea. The carved side loses weight and the block tilts, so one stroke decides both the fish’s runway and which way the world leans. Carving less scores more.
