Cat Condo Courier — Support

Thanks for playing Cat Condo Courier, a drop-and-merge delivery tower. Tap the drop bar and the head of the queue falls down a chute; because fall speed is set by rank, two crates dropped close together stay a fixed distance apart forever. A same-breed same-rank pair opens a bracket, a tap merges them, and every merge lights another room on the tower and hands back a chute slot — which is what buys back the jam a double-drop causes.
