Volcano Merge — Support

Thanks for playing Volcano Merge, a one-tap number merging puzzle. Tap where matching numbers touch to fuse the lava blobs floating in a volcano's throat. Any blob you did not touch in a merge loses heat that turn, and a blob that runs cold hardens into rock that can never move again — twenty-four of them plug the throat and end the run.
