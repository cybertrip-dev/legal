Dream Drawer — Support

Thanks for playing Dream Drawer, a bedside observation puzzle where the line you draw is a material rather than a tool. You draw a single stroke on the paper and the sleeping creatures read its slope and its material and start using it on their own. Drawing it is not the finished move: only the stretches that actually get walked wear down, and wear rewrites the physics of that stretch, so glass polishes until things begin to slide on it, chalk erodes until it breaks, rope stretches and sags, and jelly is trodden into a floor you can stand on. Because wear is per-stretch, the middle of one line can turn slippery while both ends still hold. Erasing returns all of the ink but none of the wear, and redrawing the same material in the same place resumes from the traffic already recorded there, so the same line can never be drawn twice.
