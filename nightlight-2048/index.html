Nightlight 2048 — Support

Thanks for playing Nightlight 2048, a sliding-merge puzzle in which visibility is the resource. A single oil lamp stands on a table in a dark room, and most of the board is sunk in that dark: the only tiles you can read are the ones that still hold light, and light burns down with every move you make. The sliding and merging rules are the familiar ones — what has been taken away is the assumption that you can see the board. In exchange you are given three verbs the original never had: buying light, selling it, and writing down what you have deduced. It is not a game about reaching a number, it is a game about managing what you can still see.
