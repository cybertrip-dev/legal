Beacon Dash — Support

Thanks for playing Beacon Dash, a three-lane memory runner about learning a colour code nobody wrote down. Beacon posts stand in mirrored pairs outside the corridor and light the same colour on both sides to name which of the three lanes is open on that row. The colour-to-lane codebook is written down nowhere: you observe it in the lit stretches and then run on colour alone through the dark ones. A collapse line follows at your own speed, so a mistake is not death but a trade — you pay a fraction of your headroom, and in exchange the colour you hit is permanently revealed on the code bar.
