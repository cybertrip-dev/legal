Snowball Commute — Support

Thanks for playing Snowball Commute, a one-hand downhill runner about being exactly the right size. You are inside a snowball rolling down a town before dawn, swiping between lanes and tapping to jump. The ball's left and right radii grow independently: snow fattens both sides, and a clearing strip shaves the side you hug. Every few hundred metres a window gate demands two things at once — narrow enough to fit the opening, and heavy enough to break the drift piled in the doorway — so growing is not simply an advantage, and the run is a plan about which side to thin.
