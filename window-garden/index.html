Window Garden — Support

Thanks for playing Window Garden, a cozy arcade gardening game. Six pots on a windowsill and five skies — sun, rain, mist, wind, night — with only ever one sky at a time. Each pot wants a different one, so somebody is always waiting. A bloom can be picked for 3.2 seconds; miss it and it falls, and a pot left too long under the wrong sky costs a leaf.
