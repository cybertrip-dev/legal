ARC ACE — Support

Thanks for playing ARC ACE, a one-tap neon arcade basketball game. The cannon at the bottom sweeps its firing angle back and forth; tap anywhere to launch a neon ball on a parabolic arc and swish it through the bouncing hoop. One miss ends the run, so keep your combo alive and chase your high score.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email us at info@cybertrip.jp. We usually reply within a few business days.
