DEVELOP — Support

Thanks for playing DEVELOP, a one-tap nonogram puzzle game. Use the number clues along each row and column to work out which squares belong to the hidden picture, then tap them to fill them in. Three wrong taps ruin the print, and a completed grid develops into a colored pixel photo with a rarity rating.
