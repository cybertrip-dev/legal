Bento Belts — Support

Thanks for playing Bento Belts, an ordering puzzle on a belt you are not allowed to stop. Thirteen plates ride a loop of conveyor belt that never stops and cannot be paused, and the counter serves whatever arrives on its own. Your only move is to swap two neighbouring plates as they pass, so you are not choosing what to serve — you are composing the order in which things will arrive at a counter that is already running. Everything you want to fix has to be fixed before it comes around, and the sequence you leave behind is the entire score.
