TIMES TAP — Support

Thanks for playing TIMES TAP, a one-tap neon shooting-gallery game built on times tables. Numbered targets rise toward your aim line under a rule like "multiples of 7" that switches as you play. Tap to shoot the numbers that match, hold your fire on the ones that do not, and one wrong shot or missed multiple ends the run.
