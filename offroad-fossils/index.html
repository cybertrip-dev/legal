Offroad Fossils — Support

Thanks for playing Offroad Fossils, a short-run driving game where digging is a landing rather than a dig. The screen is split: the top half is the ridge you drive, the bottom half is the strata directly beneath it, showing how deep each fossil is buried and the shape of every hole you have already made. Three pedals are the whole input, and they swap meaning in the air: on the ground they are throttle, brake and a drill charge, and airborne they pitch the truck nose-down, nose-up and lock its rotation. Depth is settled at the instant of impact with no dice involved — it comes from the angle between the truck and the slope it lands on, so a fast landing at the wrong angle only breaks the truck. What you dig up fills a museum you can walk through.
