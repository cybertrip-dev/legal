JELLY SUMO — Support

Thanks for playing JELLY SUMO, a one-finger neon sumo arcade game. Drag sideways to wind up your jelly wrestler, release to shove, and knock your opponent out of the neon ring. Every win extends your streak and brings out a heavier rival.
