Sticker Factory — Support

Thanks for playing Sticker Factory, a 90-second sticker-sheet packing shift. Drag die-cut sticker shapes from a three-slot tray onto a 5x5 sheet. The sheet only ships — and only pays — when all 25 slots are full, so a sheet with a gap in it is worth exactly nothing. Landing a die against a sticker of the same drawn pattern bonds it and builds a chain, and the biggest single-pattern block on a shipped sheet sets its bonus multiplier.
