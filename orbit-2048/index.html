Orbit 2048 — Support

Thanks for playing Orbit 2048, a one-finger number-merging puzzle. The board is three concentric rings of eight sectors instead of a grid. You spin a ring with your finger rather than sliding rows, gravity points at the centre, and only equal numbers lined up along a radius merge — never two side by side on the same ring. Turning one ring always turns another the opposite way, so every move is a choice of which direction to travel, not just where to end up.
