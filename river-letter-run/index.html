River Letter Run — Support

Thanks for playing River Letter Run, a one-tap river crossing where a letter is worth nothing until you reach the bank. You hop upstream one square at a time across drifting logs and lily pads. Stepping on a letter tile plants it on your back like a flag, but nothing happens at that moment — not the score, not the word slots, not the flood. Value settles in the single frame you land on the bank, where the whole load is cashed at once and a completed word shoves the oncoming flood back several rows. Drown before you bank and the entire load washes away. Pick-up order does not matter, so the decision is never which letter to take but whether to run for the bank with what you already carry or reach for one more.
