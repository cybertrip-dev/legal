Quilt Kingdom — Support

Thanks for playing Quilt Kingdom, a patchwork puzzle where a bad seam fails later rather than now. You sew fabric patches together to spread a kingdom's quilt, and a seam only holds along edges whose patterns actually agree. A mismatched edge still lets you place the patch and still looks like progress, which is the trap: it holds until the next frost, several turns later, and then that stretch tears away and takes the land behind it. So the game is not about fitting a shape into a gap but about how much delayed risk you are willing to sew into the border you are about to build on.
