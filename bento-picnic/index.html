Bento Picnic — Support

Thanks for playing Bento Picnic, a seating puzzle seen from directly above a lacquer lunch box. You cannot summon a guest and you cannot choose who comes next; the only thing you choose is which compartment to offer, and whether that guest sits down at all depends on who is already sitting in the four compartments around it. A guest who does sit eats the food in that compartment and leaves the space empty behind them, so every success makes the board a little emptier and the next placement a little harder. It is a puzzle about seating order and neighbours rather than about choosing the right bait.
