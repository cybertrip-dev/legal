Domino Orchard — Support

Thanks for playing Domino Orchard, a one-stroke chain-reaction puzzle. Draw a single unbroken line and it becomes a row of standing dominoes across the orchard. Tap any one of them to topple it, and tapping the middle sends the chain running both directions at once. Fruit trees relay the chain onward as they fall, there is no timer and nothing is lost on a failure, and a run that stops short shows exactly how far the chain did not reach.
