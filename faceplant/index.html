FACEPLANT FLIP — Support

Thanks for playing FACEPLANT FLIP, a one-hold arcade gymnastics game. Your ragdoll gymnast launches off the springboard automatically. Hold to spin faster, release just before upright so the rotation coasts to a stop, and land feet-first on the mat instead of face-first.
