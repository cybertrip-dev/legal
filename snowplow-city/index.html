Snowplow City — Support

Thanks for playing Snowplow City, a 120-second plough territory game. Drive a plough through a night city. A block only lights up when all four of its sides are cleared and the cleared road still connects back to the snow dump. Four-way swipes steer; the plough never stops. Open the target number of blocks before dawn at 120 seconds.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email us at info@cybertrip.jp. We usually reply within a few business days.
