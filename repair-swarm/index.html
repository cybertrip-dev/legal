Repair Swarm — Support

Thanks for playing Repair Swarm, an arcade game about how thick a swarm needs to be. You repaint a corroding circuit board with a swarm of small drones, and a pad is only permanently repaired when a sufficiently thick swarm finishes it. A pad finished by a thin swarm becomes a temporary patch instead: it peels away after a few seconds, never counts toward recovery, and the corrosion walks straight across it. Tapping splits the swarm into two lanes, covering roughly seventy percent more board while halving the thickness of each lane. Split or stay whole is the entire decision.
