Campfire Tales — Support

Thanks for playing Campfire Tales, a collecting game where the order you collect in is the puzzle. Every spark rising off the fire is a word. Tapping three ripe sparks makes a key out of the order you caught them in, and that key selects which tale gets told — the same three words caught in a different order produce a different story with a different ending. So the play is not about catching as many sparks as possible; it is about deliberately letting the spark in front of you go and waiting for the one that belongs next. All twenty-four tales are reachable without paying anything.
