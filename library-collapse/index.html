Library Collapse — Support

Thanks for playing Library Collapse, a one-tap tidying puzzle. Books are piled on a returns desk after closing. Tapping a cluster of the same genre collapses it, which scores nothing on its own — points come from what falls afterwards, when a column happens to end up stepping up in size from the bottom, paperback then pocket then hardback. A column that forms that staircase lights up and slides itself onto the shelf. You act on colour; the board resolves on height.
