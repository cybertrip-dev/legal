Island Stitch — Support

Thanks for playing Island Stitch, a shipping-chart puzzle whose main verb is cutting, not drawing. You link islands on a sea chart with shipping lines and try to keep the company's ledger in the black. Every line you draw charges upkeep every single season, for as long as it exists, and the cargo moves to different islands each season — so a network that was ideal three seasons ago is now a bill you are paying for nothing. Lines are a liability rather than an asset, the best chart is the smallest one that still reaches the cargo, and most of your real decisions are about which route to tear out.
