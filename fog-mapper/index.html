Fog Mapper — Support

Thanks for playing Fog Mapper, a survey game about walking a fogged island with one lantern. You plant stakes as you walk, and closing a loop turns all the fog inside it into chart paper at once. The paper is not a score, it is fuel: your lantern burns roughly ten times faster over open fog than over ground you have surveyed yourself. Mistcatchers cannot set foot on charted ground, and standing on your own chart is the only way to cut fresh stakes. Surveying is therefore not a progress bar but the act of physically enlarging the region in which you are able to stay alive.
