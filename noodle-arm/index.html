NOODLE STRETCH — Support

Thanks for playing NOODLE STRETCH, a one-hold arcade gap-crossing game. Hold to stretch your slime's rubbery body toward the next ledge in a glowing cave, then release to grab on. Grip the very edge for a STRETCH bonus and build a combo, but come up short and you slip off, or reach too far and your body snaps.
