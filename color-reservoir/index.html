Color Reservoir — Support

Thanks for playing Color Reservoir, an arithmetic pipe puzzle. Each tank carries a volume. Route a pipe to its outlet spending that volume exactly — a cell one step uphill costs two. Capacity is not the same as distance, so the board is a sum rather than a maze, and no two pipes may cross.
