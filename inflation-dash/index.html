HYPERINFLATE — Support

Thanks for playing HYPERINFLATE, a one-tap arcade price-timing game. Tap to buy the moment the swinging price needle hits its floor, chain exact buys for a bigger combo multiplier, and keep your wallet alive as the floor price ratchets permanently upward. Every bottom you let pass raises the floor for good, and the run ends when your money is gone or the floor catches the MSRP ceiling.
