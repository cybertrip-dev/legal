Ghost Lane — Support

Thanks for playing Ghost Lane, a swipe-only three-lane arcade runner. Swipe sideways to change lane and swipe up to jump. The lanes you stood in on your previous run come back as walls on the next one, so improving means leaving your future self a way through rather than simply running faster.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email us at info@cybertrip.jp. We usually reply within a few business days.

Subscription
