COIL UP — Support

Thanks for playing COIL UP, a one-finger pottery arcade game. Drag one finger left and right to control how fast the clay coil winds onto the spinning wheel, keeping your speed inside the green tempo band so each ring stacks evenly. Wind too fast and the clay snaps, too slow and it overlaps — finish all 12 coils before the integrity gauge empties.
