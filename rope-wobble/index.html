TIGHTROPE WOBBLE — Support

Thanks for playing TIGHTROPE WOBBLE, a one-finger balance arcade game. Drag your finger left and right to shift the puppet's weight back over the rope, tame the overshoot before it tips past the point of no return, and walk as far down the tightrope as you can.
