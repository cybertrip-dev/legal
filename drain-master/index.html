Drain Master — Support

Thanks for playing Drain Master, a one-finger flood-clearing game. Drag a single mobile drain nozzle around a flooded street. Water flows downhill on its own, so suction is decided by where you stand rather than how fast you chase it — park at the low point of a basin and the water comes to you, stand on a rise and almost nothing reaches the intake. Leave the upstream basins too long and they overflow back into ground you already cleared.
