PEG DROP — Support

Thanks for playing PEG DROP, a one-tap neon pachinko arcade game. Tap to drop a ball from the marker sweeping across the top of the board, let it ricochet down through the pegs into a multiplier lane, and decide whether to re-drop your whole pot or tap COLLECT before an X0 lane wipes it out.
