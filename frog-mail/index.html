Frog Mail — Support

Thanks for playing Frog Mail, a quiet route-planning game about posting parcels to a traveller. You cannot steer the frog — he walks a road network on his own, and your only lever is posting a parcel to a post office ahead of him. The docket prints arrival, opening and expiry times and a verdict computed by dry-running the same simulation the round uses, so a docket marked CATCH is a fact rather than a forecast. A missed parcel sits on the counter and can be forwarded for a free second shot.
