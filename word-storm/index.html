WORD STORM — Support

Thanks for playing WORD STORM, a one-tap anagram survival game. Tap the letter tiles on the slowly spinning ring in the correct spelling order to pull each one into the center slot and lock the word. Each completed word refills your storm gauge, and the run ends when the gauge empties.
