FLICKER GAP — Support

Thanks for playing FLICKER GAP, a one-tap spot-the-difference arcade game. Eight security monitors flicker between an original scene and an altered one, with a white flash in between that hides the change. Find the single monitor that changed and tap it before the timer runs out.
