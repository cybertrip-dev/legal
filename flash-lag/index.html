FLASH LAG — Support

Thanks for playing FLASH LAG, a one-tap precision-aim arcade game. A comet orbits a dark radar dial and strobes for an instant; tap the ring where it actually flashed, not where your eyes place it. Your vision reports the light ahead of its true position, so you learn to aim slightly behind the flash, and three misses in a row end the run.
