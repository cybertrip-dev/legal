Aquarium Sweep — Support

Thanks for playing Aquarium Sweep, a 90-second merge-and-clean aquarium. One sweep sucks up grime and shoves an egg one square along to merge it. The eggs you leave on the glass throttle the filtration, so the more you hoard the further away the next clear gets — cleaning and merging are the same single action.
