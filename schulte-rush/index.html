SCHULTE RUSH — Support

Thanks for playing SCHULTE RUSH, a one-tap number-hunting speed game. Find and tap the numbered tiles in ascending order, starting from 1, across a 3x3, then 4x4, then 5x5 chalkboard grid. Clear all 50 tiles as fast as you can — a wrong tap only adds half a second, so the run never resets.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email us at info@cybertrip.jp. We usually reply within a few business days.
