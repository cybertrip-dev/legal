HIVEBREAKER — Support

Thanks for playing HIVEBREAKER, a one-finger arcade climbing game. Drag left or right to steer a pest-control drone up a tower face packed with hornet nests. Only nests matching the agent your drone is carrying dissolve on contact, so hitting any other colour jams you in place while the swarm massing below closes the gap.
