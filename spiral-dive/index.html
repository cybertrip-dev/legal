SPIRAL DIVE — Support

Thanks for playing SPIRAL DIVE, a one-finger neon arcade descent game. Drag left or right to spin the neon tower until the gap in the ring below lines up under your bouncing orb, then drop through. Chain aligned gaps for a pierce multiplier, skim the red death zones for bonus points, and see how deep you can get.
