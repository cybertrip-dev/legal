Forge 2048 — Support

Thanks for playing Forge 2048, a swipe merge puzzle about forging weapons. Swipe to slide weapons across a four-by-four anvil and merge matching pairs into the next weapon up. The direction of the swipe decides which family the merged weapon belongs to, and new stock only appears on the square the last hammer left.
