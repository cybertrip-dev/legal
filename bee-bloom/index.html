Bee Bloom — Support

Thanks for playing Bee Bloom, a one-finger territory and gardening game. Steer a bee by touching where you want it to fly. Crossing bare soil lays a pollen trail, and returning to already-bloomed ground closes the loop so everything inside it flowers at once. The shape you enclosed decides the flower you get — its corners and roundness set the petal count and form — and pollen is a fixed budget per garden with no way to refill it.
