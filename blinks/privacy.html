Privacy Policy — Blinks: Drop & Pop

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Blinks ("the app") is published by CYBERTRIP, INC. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we store on your device

Your best score, best chain, all-clear count, spark balance, unlocked collection items, equipped face, well and pop style, daily results, daily streak, an interrupted run's board, total play time and settings are saved only on your device (local storage). We do not operate a server and do not receive, sell, or share this data. There is no account and no sign-in. Every board is generated on the device, so the game is fully playable offline from the first launch. This data exists only for as long as the app is installed — deleting the app permanently deletes it, and we keep no copy.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process data such as a device advertising identifier, ad interaction data, and approximate, coarse information about your device. Depending on your choices and region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

  • On iOS, we ask for permission via App Tracking Transparency (ATT) before any tracking for personalized ads. If you decline, you still see ads, but non-personalized ones.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent dialog is shown before any ads are served. Personalized ads are shown only with your consent; otherwise ads are non-personalized.
  • Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can remove the full-screen ad and the banner with the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Rewarded ads are always optional, are never required to play, and are never required to progress: everything in the collection can be earned by playing, and the reward is granted whether or not the ad actually plays.

Purchases

The app sells one product: Remove Ads (blinks.removeads), a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It removes the full-screen ad and the banner for good and adds the Supporter face. It is not a subscription and there is nothing recurring to cancel. It grants no sparks and unlocks no other collection item — those are earned by playing only. Billing is processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit — we never receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy

Collection items unlocked by playing are kept permanently, including if the purchase is later refunded.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset the advertising identifier in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app to delete all locally stored game data.
