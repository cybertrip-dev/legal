Blinks: Drop & Pop — Support

Thanks for playing Blinks, a one-thumb puzzle game with no timer. Press anywhere, slide left or right to pick a column, and release to drop an orb. Four or more of the same colour touching will blink, pop, and everything above collapses — which can set off another pop, and another. Keep the stack away from the red line at the top.

How to play

  • One input. Press, slide, release. Lifting your finger away from the board cancels and costs nothing, so it is always safe to look.
  • Nothing moves until you drop. There is no clock and no reaction test anywhere in the game.
  • A full column cannot be selected, so you can never lose by mis-tapping.
  • Chains are worth far more than separate clears. Five clears in a chain pay 600; five separate clears pay 200.
  • The eye under the floor eats what your chains feed it. Five orbs and it blinks, swallowing the bottom row of the board so everything sinks one row. The row it ate comes back as the next row pushed up from below.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email us at info@cybertrip.jp. We usually reply within a few business days.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time, non-consumable purchase — not a subscription, and there is nothing recurring to cancel. It removes the full-screen ad and the banner and adds the Supporter face. Reward videos stay available if you want them; they are always optional and never required to play or to progress.

To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings → Restore purchases inside the game while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Progress and data

Everything is saved on your device only. There is no account, no sign-in and no server, and the game works fully offline. Deleting the app deletes your scores, streak and collection permanently, and we keep no copy.

Accessibility

Each orb colour also carries a distinct pupil shape, so colour is never the only way to tell them apart. Settings offers high-contrast pupils, reduced motion, and switches for sound and haptics. The game is fully playable with the sound off.
