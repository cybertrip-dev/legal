Rush-Hour Relay — Support

Thanks for playing Rush-Hour Relay, a tap-and-swipe arcade road-crossing game. Tap to hop one row forward and swipe to move sideways or back off. Read the countdown on each traffic signal, land while the light is green or amber, and chain those landings into a multiplier up to x5 as you cross the city.
