Ink Reef — Support

Thanks for playing Ink Reef, a seabed drawing puzzle scored only where two colours meet. You carve channels into the sand of the seabed and ink wells up and runs down them under gravity. Painted area is worth nothing whatsoever. The only thing that scores is a confluence: when two streams of different colours arrive in the same cell, a coral grows there that neither colour could ever produce alone. So the puzzle is never coverage — it is arranging slopes and timings so that the right two colours reach the same square, and the reef you end up with is a record of the meetings you managed to engineer.
