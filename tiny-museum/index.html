Tiny Museum — Support

Thanks for playing Tiny Museum, a short excavation puzzle about joining broken things back together. You dig soil from the top down, and a cell can only be dug once the cell directly above it is already open, so the dig front descends column by column and choosing which column to lower is the whole plan. Relics are joined by matching the direction their broken faces point, and rotating one is free and unlimited while every swing of the pick is counted against you. Wet soil takes two swings and a sealed clod will not open until the cells above and to either side of it are clear. What you assemble fills the drawers and plinths of a museum that persists between runs.
