GUILT TIP — Support

Thanks for playing GUILT TIP, a one-tap arcade game about tipping under pressure. The card reader swivels toward you and the suggested tip climbs from 18% to 30%, and one tap locks in whatever rate is showing — the lower you settle, the more you score. Stall too long and the cashier's stare meter fills and you are DECLINED; settle high too often and your wallet gauge drains and you go BROKE.
