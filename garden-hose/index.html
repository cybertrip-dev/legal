Garden Hose — Support

Thanks for playing Garden Hose, a one-finger irrigation puzzle about the order things dry in. You have one hose and a garden that dries at different rates bed by bed. Drag to aim the water, and the beds you are not watering keep drying while you do it, so the game is about reading which bed will be the next to go rather than about watering the driest one. Later gardens add beds that want less water rather than more, so the same hose has to be moved off a plot before it is finished.
