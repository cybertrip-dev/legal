Trash Kraken — Support

Thanks for playing Trash Kraken, an eat-and-grow sea-cleaning arcade game. A vortex drags every piece of litter it touches toward your mouth, but only litter smaller than your rank gets through — so the tyre that shouldered you aside two ranks ago is suddenly lunch, and every rank costs you speed. Swimming leaves a wake that carries litter along your own path, so the game is about writing a current and then eating the pile.
