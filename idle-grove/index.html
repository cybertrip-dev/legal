IDLE GROVE — Support

Thanks for playing IDLE GROVE, a one-tap cozy pixel-art idle game. Tap to shake your trees and knock the ripe fruit loose, timing each tap to the peak of the sway for a double-value PERFECT SHAKE. Fruit keeps ripening while the app is closed, and you spend the seeds you harvest on new trees before burning the whole grove for ash that permanently speeds up regrowth.
