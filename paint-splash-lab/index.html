Paint Splash Lab — Support

Thanks for playing Paint Splash Lab, a two-colour print-shop puzzle where the aim unfolds the board. You fire ink droplets from a nozzle at a printing plate, and the droplet mirrors perfectly off every wall, painting its trail as it goes — the area it covers is the finished print. Aiming is the trick: as you drag, the plate hinges on the first wall the current heading would strike and unfolds outward into as many as three sheets, and on that unfolded paper a ricochet is simply a straight line. Let go and the paper folds back, and the droplet flies the real bent path you just drew straight. Ink is limited, so a shot is judged by how much of the outline it fills rather than by where it lands.
