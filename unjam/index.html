UNJAM — Support

Thanks for playing UNJAM, a one-finger neon parking-lot puzzle game. Drag one car at a time to clear a lane, then slide the red HERO car out the exit on the right. Solve each packed 6x6 lot in as few moves as you can and dispatch as many cars as possible before the shift ends.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email us at info@cybertrip.jp. We usually reply within a few business days.
