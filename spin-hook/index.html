Spin Hook — Support

Thanks for playing Spin Hook, a one-button climbing game about the area a rope sweeps. The rope is a harvesting tool rather than a way to travel. Press and a hook flies to the nearest anchor you have not used yet; hold and the rope reels in, and the fan the rope sweeps out is what actually collects the motes spiralling around that anchor. Release and you launch along the tangent with no air control at all, so a climb is nothing but a sequence of grab radii and release angles, run just ahead of the rot rising from the bottom of the screen.
