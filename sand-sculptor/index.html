Sand Sculptor — Support

Thanks for playing Sand Sculptor, a sculpting game in which the sand is conserved. One nozzle sucks and spits sand: with your finger in the sand it draws, with your finger in the air it deposits, so digging, heaping, shaving and smoothing all happen in a single stroke with no mode button anywhere. Sand is never created or destroyed — drawing it in is only loading, and the only thing that scores is how closely the sand you put down matches the target silhouette on the plinth. The amount you suck up is worth nothing at all, and material you shave off returns to the tank rather than being lost.
