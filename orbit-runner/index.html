Orbit Runner — Support

Thanks for playing Orbit Runner, a game about holding on to a turning rock and choosing the moment to let go. You latch onto a spinning asteroid and it carries you at its own angular speed, throwing away whatever speed you arrived with. You swipe to name the rock you are aiming for, then tap to release along whatever tangent you happen to be standing on at that instant. Every release also stamps you with the rule of the rock you just left, and that rule overwrites exactly one number on your next catch, so deciding where to fly is the same act as deciding what you will be carrying when you land. Nothing is ever cut or destroyed; a closing belt of dust supplies the pressure from the outside in.
