LOOP CAPTURE — Support

Thanks for playing LOOP CAPTURE, a one-finger neon territory-capture arcade game. Drag to steer your cursor out from your own territory, close the loop back into it to claim everything you enclosed, and dodge the stingers that cut your trail before the 20-second round ends.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email us at info@cybertrip.jp. We usually reply within a few business days.
